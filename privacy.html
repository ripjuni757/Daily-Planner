SOLENARY
ACCOUNT AND USAGE DATA

Privacy and usage notice

Solenary is a personal planning service. The person operating Solenary can see account registration email addresses, registration dates, and last sign-in dates through the secured administrator dashboard.

Automatic active-use analytics

When you're signed in and actively using the Solenary website, it automatically records approximate minutes of activity. Tracking pauses while the browser tab is hidden, unfocused or idle. Daily totals are associated with your account so the administrator can understand usage and improve the service. No separate opt-in prompt is used for this feature.

We don't include task titles, notes, email messages, page paths, keystrokes, or mouse coordinates in these analytics records. Times are approximate, and historical minutes from before this feature's launch are not available.

Optional Google Calendar integration

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Solenary requests permission to view and edit calendar events and list your calendars. Solenary imports event titles, dates, times, and locations into your personal planner. With your separate approval, edits and deletions of linked Solenary tasks are also sent to your selected Google calendar. A private server function stores an encrypted Google refresh token so you do not need to reauthorize at every visit. Google passwords are never collected.

Calendar information is used only to provide synchronization requested by the connected user, not for advertising, profiling, or resale. We do not use Google Calendar event contents in the administrator usage dashboard. You can disconnect within Integrations; this removes Solenary's stored Google authorization and connection mappings, while keeping your existing Solenary tasks. You can additionally revoke access through your Google Account permissions. Google data held as Solenary tasks remains subject to your account's delete/export controls.

Solenary's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs should comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. Before public launch, the operator should verify full compliance, provide a privacy contact, and complete any required Google OAuth verification.

Access and storage

Usage totals are stored in Supabase and are accessible only through administrator-checked endpoints. Ordinary users do not have access to other accounts' email addresses or usage totals. Account data and usage metrics are kept until they are deleted or the account is removed, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Your information

In Settings, you can delete your previously recorded usage history. This does not disable future activity tracking while using Solenary. If you need access, correction, deletion, or another legally required privacy choice, contact the site operator using the website's feedback form. Applicable privacy rights vary by location and age.

Before using Solenary publicly, the operator should provide a dedicated privacy contact and review the requirements that apply to the service and its audience, including minors.
